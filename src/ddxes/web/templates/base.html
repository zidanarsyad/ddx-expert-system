DDX-ES is an explainable expert system for differential diagnosis and triage. It reasons over an OWL knowledge graph with rules drawn from published clinical guidelines and decision rules, on the synthetic DDXPlus patients (Fansi Tchango et al. 2022).

An educational prototype for the course Sistem Pakar Lanjut, Universitas Gadjah Mada. It is not a medical device.
